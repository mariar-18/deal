# DEAL

DEAL is a web based marketplace designed for students to buy and sell items within their campus. It allows students to list items, browse available products, negotiate prices, arrange pickup, and manage their purchases in one place. The platform supports both buyers and sellers, with features built around the way students actually exchange used or unwanted items on campus.

## Live Demo
https://deal-production-9dc9.up.railway.app/

## Description

DEAL is designed around the everyday buying and selling needs of students. Many students have books, electronics, accessories, study materials, and other useful items that they no longer need, while other students may be looking for the same things at a reasonable price. DEAL provides a dedicated space where these exchanges can happen within the student community.

A seller can provide details about an item and manage the responses they receive, while a buyer can make a direct purchase or negotiate before deciding. The bargaining system also allows both sides to agree on a suitable price and a convenient pickup arrangement.

DEAL also goes beyond regular product listings through features such as seller ratings, and the Wanted Board. Together, these features make it easier for students to find useful items, communicate their requirements, and complete exchanges in a more organized way.

## Buyer Features
1. Create a buyer account
2. Browse and view product details
3. Add items to cart
4. Buy items directly
5. Send bargain offers to sellers
6. Suggest up to three preferred pickup locations, dates, and times
7. Track active bargains
8. View completed orders
9. Rate sellers after a purchase
10. Post requests for items through the Wanted Board

## Seller Features
1. Create a seller account
2. List new items for sale
3. Set price, category, condition, and description
4. View and manage listed items
5. Receive bargain offers
6. Accept or reject offers
7. Suggest a custom pickup option when responding to an offer
8. Track sold items
9. View buyer requests through the Wanted Board
